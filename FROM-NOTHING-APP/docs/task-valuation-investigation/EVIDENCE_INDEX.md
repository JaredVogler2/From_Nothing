# Evidence index — task-valuation investigation

**Source revision:** `5d1236c8e5777b9075a652571707ae57bf5357ff` (`main`, 2026-11-02). Reports 01–09 changed no application code, tests, policies, schemas or UI. On the owner's direction, per-shift goals of record were then implemented as D-26 (`09` §7; commits 55e857e to 0e12945, with the simulation's cutoff gap and floor behaviors). Report 10 adds one read-only tool, `tools/calibrate_scores.py`, with its tests.

**Data:** all experiments use the synthetic `FleetSpec` fleet (`focus/data/fullscale_fixture.py`). Money is a modeled daily rate per customer airline and minor model (`fixtures/economics/README.md`): a planning assumption, never a contract or a realized loss. Runs made before the owner's decision of 2026-11-11 (`docs/execution/DECISIONS.md` D-31) priced a late day at the ticket revenue the aircraft would have earned, 2.7 to 5.2 times the rate used since (median 3.5), and split the synthetic fleet over two more customer and minor-model pairs. Their dollar figures were not re-run; a re-run at a later revision gives different fleets and amounts. Each fleet is one example: the real fleet is whichever aircraft have open tasks (D-32). The runs made with this folder's scripts (`study.py`, `plan_classes.py`, `shadow_sim.py`, `inversions.py`, `offplan_value.py`, `offplan_paired.py`, `known_truth.py`, `cherry_pick.py`) planned with the scheduler's first pass only (`max_moves=0`, `max_repairs=0`), to save time. The stored 50-aircraft run (`runtime/sim/fleet.db`) used three improvement moves and two repairs. `SB` below measures what the search adds on one 10-aircraft fleet: 1% of the forecast's late cost before D-31, 24% at the owner's rates. Within one experiment every floor was planned the same way, so the comparisons between floors are like for like.
