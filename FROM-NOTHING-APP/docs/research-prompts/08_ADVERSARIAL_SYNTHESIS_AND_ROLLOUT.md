# Prompt 08 — Adversarial Review, Prioritization and Rollout
Review every experiment against the original PDF claims, the root master spec and independent validator. Attack graph-memory nearest neighbors, stale cache, SAT timeouts, invalid Benders cuts, supply iteration oscillation, shifted data distributions, concurrency, hidden resource coupling, model overfitting and fabricated cost benefits.
Create an evidence-weighted scorecard for each method: accuracy/feasibility, economic lift, P95 latency, runtime/memory, complexity, maintainability, explainability, integration risk, and operational risk. Include confidence intervals, negative results and scope limitations. No adoption from one seed or synthetic-only gains without clearly stated limitations.
Propose three deployment levels: research-only, shadow-mode, and approved limited operational rollout. Every optional capability requires feature flag, audit trail, immutable snapshots, fallback, independent validation, human approval for dispatch-impacting changes, security review, and rollback test. Preserve master acceptance A01-A72 and C01-C39.
Deliver RESEARCH_DECISION_REPORT.md, INTEGRATION_ROADMAP.md, architecture diffs, rollback runbook, benchmark artifact index and explicit go/no-go per method. Do not modify live SQL, publish schedules, or declare real-world gains.
